## 2026-10-08: Pay days, and one list to pick from

- **A bell for what's new.** A bell in the sidebar (on a phone, the first row of More) lists the releases you haven't seen and the Nudges Noodle sent you, newest first, each leading to what it is about. A dot shows while something is unread; opening the bell reads it, on your phone and your computer alike. A Nudge you dismissed can be found there again, and it is listed even if you never turned Nudges on for a device. The Nudge about an update now names the release and opens it here.
- On Plan › Income each Parent can say how they are paid: on a salary, with what one paycheck usually is and its pay days.
- A paycheck counts in the month of the pay day it is for, whatever day the bank posted it. One posted on the last day of a month for the 1st is the new month's Income, and a line of Income can say which pay day it is for.
- A card statement that writes purchases as positive amounts comes in as spending.
- Owed back on Transactions is a small section: one line says who owes how much, with each purchase on a line under it.
- The Plan's Commitments are grouped by what each one is: Credit cards, then Loans, then Bills.
- Every list a Bucket, bill or Goal is picked from is the same list, each Bucket with its colour and letter as in the Plan.
- Household settings has three tabs: Settings, Logs and this Changelog.
- A card payment is said one way everywhere: "Card payment" is what it is, "It's a card payment" says so, and Review names the card and whether paying it is spending. A payment whose bank wording names no card asks which card first when it may be one of yours.
- A paycheck that counts on its pay day is listed, and counted in Money in, on the Transactions page of the month it counts in.
- A charge that names a lender you have a loan with is offered in Review as a payment on that loan, and a Rule sends each later payment to the loan whose payment it is.
- A loan's page lists each month's payment against its schedule, and a loan that is paid off stops being planned from the next month.
- Reports › People shows where the money went for every Member, Parents too, with Everyone on its own, and a Bucket's page can be narrowed by who it was for.
- Review offers who a Transaction was For when the same merchant was For the same person before.
- A Parent on a salary can be paid every two weeks or weekly, counted from any one pay day you pick. A month with one pay day more than most says the pay above your take-home pay shows as Extra income.
- A pay day whose paycheck hasn't come in five days after it is said on This Month and at the Check-in, until the pay is in.
- The download's money-in.csv has a Pay day column, so the month each paycheck counts in is in your download.
- Where a line of Income asks whose pay it is, the pay day it is for is asked with it, above Done.

## 2026-10-07: Refunds, whose Account it is, and a shorter Free to Spend

- Money into checking that reads as a refund or a reversal waits in Review with Refund suggested, instead of counting as Income.
- The Income page says each figure once and lists each deposit once, with one way to change what it counts as.
- Accounts are listed by whose they are: yours first, then the other Parent's, then the Household's. You say whose an Account is when you add it or on its page.
- A Transaction can be moved to another day. The bank's own date stays beside it, so the bank never puts it back or brings it in twice.
- Free to Spend on This Month is the figure and one short line. The working opens under "How this is worked out".
- Paying a card whose purchases you add by hand is a Transfer, and Review offers that first.
- Each credit card on Accounts says how paying it counts, what was paid to it this month and what waits in Review.

## 2026-10-06: Kinds of money in, the Log and the Docs

- Money in has a kind: Income, Refund, Paid back, Transfer or Between us. Money sent person to person waits in Review instead of counting as Income.
- Money someone pays back can be put against the purchases they owe on, and each one's Bucket or Commitment gets its money back in the month it arrives.
- A bill that varies can be set to "About": it shows its average and range.
- A bank fee or interest is offered a Fees and interest Bucket in Review.
- Every change to the Plan, with Rules, snapshots, Fresh starts and Bank Connections, is one table called the Log in Household settings. Pages link to it with "See what changed".
- There is a Docs page anyone can read, with a search.
- The account menu has a Theme choice of Light, Dark or Device.
- An open Noodle notices when it was updated, refreshes itself once nothing is being typed or waiting to save, and says "Noodle was updated" once.

## 2026-10-05: Rename, sort and delete Transactions

- Any Transaction can be renamed. One from your bank keeps the bank's wording underneath.
- Transactions can be selected and deleted together, one delete has an Undo, and a deleted bank line does not come back when the bank syncs.
- Transactions can be put in order by Name, Assigned to and Account as well as Date and Amount.
- Connecting a bank asks how far back to bring in what you spent.
- Review offers a credit card payment as a card payment that isn't spending, and typing a name no Bucket has offers to create that Bucket.
- A Commitment can be set to pay down a card or loan.
- Money one of you sent the other can be marked "Between us".
- An Account you no longer use can be archived and restored, and each Parent can change their own name and colour.

## 2026-10-04: Snapshots, new colours and More on a phone

- Noodle takes a snapshot of your Household every night, and each snapshot in Household settings has a Restore button.
- Start fresh takes a snapshot first, which can be put back for up to 90 days.
- A Transaction waiting in Review can be filed without a Bucket.
- Extra income can be added to Free to Spend in one tap, and a few dollars over your usual pay no longer asks you to decide anything.
- Noodle has new colours: a near-white page with white cards in light, near-black in dark.
- On a phone the tab bar's last item is More, which opens every other page, Household settings, your account and Sign out.
- A credit card perk keeps the value its benefits page states, how often it renews and the uses you mark by hand.

## 2026-10-03: Filing in the background, and your data to keep

- New Transactions from a statement, a bank sync, a Quick Add or a forwarded Receipt are filed in the background about a minute after they arrive, with no page open.
- Opening Noodle goes straight to This Month.
- Inviting the other Parent gives a link to send them, which works for 7 days.
- A Parent can disconnect a bank from Accounts. Its Accounts stay, with their Transactions.
- A Parent can download the Household's data from Household settings.
- A Rule can file a merchant to a Commitment as well as a Bucket.
- Suggested Commitments are only real bills, like rent, a car loan, insurance, utilities, phone and subscriptions.

## 2026-10-02: Review one card at a time

- Review sorts one card at a time: a stack you can swipe, with Skip and Undo.
- The Plan has one header and one row of tabs, and only the page below them changes.
- On a phone This Month opens on Free to Spend, with what needs doing in one To do strip under it.
- The Plan has an Add Buckets sheet with a starter list, without the Buckets the Plan already has.
- Setup keeps your place, so it can be picked up where it was left.
- A guess Noodle is less sure of is kept and shown in Review, with why.

## 2026-10-01: A tidier desktop

- A card or loan can be paid off with a payoff Goal.
- A bank's accounts can be paired with the Accounts already there, and a Bank Connection says what it has brought in.
- This Month says each thing once, and has a right-hand column on a wide screen.
- The Glossary opens from a help icon.
- Every row of tabs looks and works the same.

## 2026-09-30: Accounts get their own area

- Accounts have their own area.
- A card statement says what it ends owing, whichever way round the bank writes it.
- A new Household's pages say what they are for and where to begin.
- Reports no longer compare against months before the Household's history.
- A Goal's history is listed by when things happened, month by month.

## 2026-09-29: Banks, Receipts and the Check-in

- An Account can be connected to its bank, which brings in its Transactions every day, pending charges included.
- Forwarded Receipts come in with their Splits made for you.
- A purchase can be added by taking a picture or by speaking.
- Setup drafts your first Plan.
- There is a weekly Check-in.
- Insights point out what is worth a look, and one can be tried in Explore.
- Each Bucket has a page of its own.

## 2026-09-28: The first Noodle

- Sign in, create a Household and invite the other Parent.
- A Plan with Buckets, Pace and Free to Spend, and Commitments for the bills.
- Quick Add, a list of Transactions you can edit, and Splits.
- Children, and spending marked as For one of them.
- A Personal Allowance for each Parent, private to them.
- Goals, and covering a Bucket that went over.
- Statements can be uploaded, and Review and Rules file what comes in.
- Reports, Ask, Explore and Nudges.
