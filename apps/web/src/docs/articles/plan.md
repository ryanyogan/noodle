The Plan is where you decide what a month's money is for. It starts from your Take-home pay and divides it across Commitments, Buckets and Goals. Whatever is left is Free to Spend. Each month's Plan starts as a copy of the month before, so you only touch it when something changes.

## Where to find it

On a computer, **Plan** is in the sidebar under Planning. On a phone, open **More**, then **Plan**.

The Plan has five pages, shown as tabs: **Overview**, **Income**, **Commitments**, **Goal funding** and **Year**. The header names the month, and lets you step to the month before or after.

## Overview

The Overview is the whole Plan on one page, top to bottom.

- **Where take-home pay goes.** One sentence, one bar, and the parts that make it up, ending in Free to Spend. On a phone, **Show the parts** opens the rest.
- **Things to check.** Shown only when something needs you. See below.
- **Buckets.** Every Bucket with its allowance, what is spent and what is left. This is where you add and change them.
- **Personal Allowances.** Each Parent's own, in a short list of their own.
- **See what changed.** A link to the Log in Household settings, showing the changes that take effect in this month.

A brand-new Household sees **Set up the Plan** here: a short list that takes you through Take-home pay, Commitments, Buckets and a Goal.

## Income

The Income page holds your **Take-home pay** and the income recorded for the month.

Take-home pay is your usual monthly pay after taxes and deductions: what lands in your account. If someone's pay varies, enter the amount you can count on, the lowest it usually is. A better month then shows the difference as Extra income, and you decide where it goes. The Plan is never built on money that might not come.

To change it:

1. Open **Plan**, then **Income**.
2. Choose **Edit take-home pay**, the small button beside the amount.
3. Type the new amount.
4. Under **Applies to**, choose how far the change reaches.
5. Save.

In a low month, Noodle offers **Lower take-home pay to** the amount that arrived. That changes that one month only, and lowers its Free to Spend by the same amount. Noodle never lowers it by itself.

## How far a change reaches

When you change an amount in the Plan, you choose one of two.

- **From October on.** That month and every later month, unless a later month has its own amount. Use it for a pay rise or a rent increase.
- **Just October.** That one month. November goes back to the amount before. Use it for a one-off, like a big birthday.

The app shows the real month's name. An amount that differs from the month before is marked "Changed this month · was $900".

## Things to check

Noodle calls these warnings Plan health. Each one links to its fix.

- Free to Spend goes below zero in a month ahead.
- Income is behind your Take-home pay.
- A Bucket is over its allowance most months.
- A Goal will not be reached by its date.

## Year

The Year page shows every month of the year in one table, from Take-home pay down to Free to Spend. Months that are over, or under way, also show what actually happened. **Lumpy months**, where a yearly bill or a third payment lowers Free to Spend, are marked "Lumpy" and listed under the table, so you see them coming.

## Good to know

- A month that has ended can no longer be changed. Its Plan stays as a record.
- To try a change before you make it, use [Explore](/docs/explore). It works on a copy of the Plan.
- Free to Spend is one number with one name everywhere on the Plan.
- If Commitments and Buckets add up to more than your Take-home pay, the Overview says by how much.
- Either Parent can change the Plan. The other Parent's Personal Allowance only ever shows in the Log as "Personal Allowance changed".
