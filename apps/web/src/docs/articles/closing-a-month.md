When a month ends, a little money is often left over. Closing is where you say what happens to it. It takes a minute, and Noodle does it for you if you forget.

## Where to find it

In the first week of a new month, This Month asks you to close the one that just ended. The section is named **Close** and the month, for example "Close September".

The [Check-in](/docs/check-in) has a Sweeps step that takes you there too.

## Close it step by step

1. Open the **Close** section. Each line is something to decide.
2. **Bucket leftovers.** Every Bucket that resets monthly and has money left gets a line. Choose a Goal to move it into, which is called a Sweep, or choose **Leave it**.
3. **Free to Spend.** If some is left, choose **Keep it in Free to Spend** or **Send to** one of your Goals. Kept, it is carried over into the next month.
4. **Extra income.** If Extra income is still waiting, choose a Goal, **Leave it in the account**, or **Decide later**.
5. Press the **Close** button with the month's name.

## If nobody closes it

If neither Parent decides within a week, Noodle closes the month by the defaults.

- Bucket leftovers go to your emergency Goal, if you have one. If you do not, they are left as they are.
- Free to Spend stays carried over into the next month.

## Afterwards

The ended month shows a section named **How** the month **ended**, for example "How September ended". It lists:

- each leftover that was Swept, and the Goal it went to
- Extra income that was sent to a Goal
- Free to Spend that was sent to a Goal, or carried over
- what each Bucket that carries over took into the next month, or that it was overspent, so the next month starts short
- who closed it: you, the other Parent, or the defaults

## Good to know

- Buckets that carry over are not part of closing. Their leftover, or their overspending, moves into the next month by itself.
- If you have no Goals yet, there is nowhere to Sweep to, so leftovers stay put.
- A Sweep moves planned money. No real money leaves an Account.
