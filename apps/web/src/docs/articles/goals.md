A Goal is a target amount you work toward over time, with a date if you like. Most Goals save: an emergency fund, a trip, braces, a new roof. A Goal can also pay off a credit card or loan. Goals are funded from Free to Spend, so they grow from money the Plan has not already promised elsewhere.

## Where to find it

On a computer, **Goals** is in the sidebar under Planning. On a phone it is a tab in the bottom bar.

The page lists Goals under **Paying off**, **Saving for**, **Completed** and **Archived**. To fund several Goals from this month's Plan in one place, open **Plan**, then **Goal funding**.

## Before you start

A Goal starts from an Account.

- A saving Goal needs a checking or savings Account, where its money is Set aside.
- A payoff Goal needs the credit card or loan, with what is owed on it today.

If you have none yet, add the Account first on **Accounts**.

## Add a saving Goal

1. Open **Goals** and choose **Add Goal**.
2. Under **What's it for?**, choose **Save up**.
3. Give it a name and a **Target**.
4. Add a **Target date** if you have one.
5. Choose the Account where the money is Set aside.
6. If some of the money is already in that Account, enter it under **Already set aside**.

## Set aside and Not set aside

Money for a Goal stays in your real Account. Noodle marks it as the Goal's. That is **Set aside**.

The part of an Account's balance that no Goal is keeping is **Not set aside**. So a savings Account with $8,000 might show $5,000 Set aside for the emergency fund and $3,000 Not set aside. You see at a glance how much of the balance is already spoken for.

## Add money to a Goal

Open the Goal and choose **Add money**. Then say where it is from.

- **From this month's plan.** Plans some of this month's Free to Spend for the Goal, and sets it aside. Free to Spend goes down by that amount.
- **Already in** the Account, shown by its name. Sets aside money the Account already has. This does not change the Plan.

Money also reaches Goals at other times: when you send Extra income to one, and when a month is closed and Bucket leftovers are Swept into one.

The Goal shows what is needed each month to reach the target by its date, and whether this month's is funded.

## Take money back or spend it

- **Take money back** stops keeping some of it for the Goal. It stays in the Account, Not set aside, free for other Goals.
- **Spend** records spending from what it has Set aside, when the day comes. It does not come out of a Bucket or Free to Spend.

## An emergency Goal

On a saving Goal, choose **Use for emergencies**. Noodle then suggests it for Extra income. When nobody decides where a month's leftovers go, they are Swept into it.

## Finish a Goal

- **Complete** it when you are done saving. Its money stays Set aside until it is spent or released.
- **Archive** it if you are no longer saving for it. Archiving releases what it has Set aside.

## Paying off a card or loan

Choose **Pay off a card or loan** when you add the Goal. What is owed today is the target, and the Goal is done when that reaches $0. Nothing is Set aside for it. You plan extra payments from Free to Spend, then pay the card or loan from checking as usual. [Paying off debt](/docs/paying-off-debt) explains the whole picture.

## Good to know

- Goals are funded from the current month's Free to Spend.
- Setting money aside, or releasing it, changes nothing in the Plan.
- If a Goal will not reach its target by its date, the Plan says so under Things to check: fund it more, or move its date.
- Each Goal keeps a History of everything added, taken back and spent.
