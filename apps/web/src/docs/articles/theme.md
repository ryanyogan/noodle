Noodle comes in a light look and a dark look. You can pick one, or let Noodle follow your phone or computer.

## Where to find it

- **On a computer:** open the account menu and look for **Theme**.
- **On a phone:** open **More**. Theme is at the foot of the sheet, with your account.

## Change the theme

1. Open the account menu, or More on a phone.
2. Under **Theme**, choose one of the three.

- **Light** is always light.
- **Dark** is always dark.
- **Device** follows your phone's or computer's own setting. If your device turns dark in the evening, Noodle does too.

The change happens at once. There is nothing to save.

## Good to know

- The choice is remembered on the device you made it on. Your phone and your computer can each have their own.
- It is your choice only. It does not change what the other Parent sees.
- These Docs follow the same theme.
