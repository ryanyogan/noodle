Not all money that lands in your account is pay. A refund, money a friend sent you, or money you moved from another account would all make the month look richer than it is. So every piece of money in has a kind, and only one kind counts as Income.

## The five kinds

- **Income** is pay or other earnings: a paycheck, a bonus, a tax refund, interest earned. Only Income counts toward your Take-home pay and Extra income.
- **Refund** is money a merchant gave back for something you bought.
- **Paid back** is money from someone outside the Household for something you bought, like a Child's other parent paying half of tuition. See [Paid back and Owed back](/docs/paid-back-and-owed-back).
- **Transfer** is your own money moving between your Accounts.
- **Between us** is money one Parent sent the other. See [Money between Parents](/docs/money-between-parents).

## Where to find it

Open **Transactions**. Money in for the month is listed under **Money in**, each line with its kind and a + before the amount. Income also has a page of its own: open **Plan**, then **Income**.

## What Noodle decides for you

- A deposit that reads as payroll is Income without asking.
- Money a person sent you (Zelle, Venmo, PayPal, Cash App, Apple Cash) is not counted as Income until you say what it is. It waits under **Money in to look at**.
- Money that reads as a refund or a reversal ("AMAZON REFUND", "PURCHASE RETURN", "FEE REVERSAL", "MERCHANT CREDIT", "CHARGEBACK") waits there too, with **Refund** first. Choose it and Noodle asks which purchase it is a Refund for.
- A tax refund and interest your bank paid you are Income.
- Any other deposit is Income, as before.
- A Rule you stated always wins.

## Change what a line is

1. Open **Transactions** and find the line under **Money in**.
2. Choose **Change what … is** on its row. Noodle asks "What is this money?"
3. Pick the kind.

If you pick **Paid back**, Noodle then asks what it is paying back.

Noodle refuses to take a line out of Income when Extra income you already decided for that month would no longer be covered. Undo that decision first. See [Extra income](/docs/extra-income).

## Whose pay

Each line of Income says whose pay it is: one Parent's, or **The Household** for interest, a tax refund, or pay nobody has said is theirs.

1. Open **Plan**, then **Income**.
2. Change **Whose pay** on the line.
3. Noodle offers "Always treat deposits from … as …'s pay". Accept, and deposits from that sender are that Parent's pay from now on.

## Pay that lands somewhere else first

When pay lands in one account and you move it to another, like Gusto into Chase, the move is a Transfer, not Income a second time. Mark it once and Noodle offers "Always treat money from Gusto into Chase as a Transfer?" Accept, and that pair is always a Transfer, whatever the days or the amount.

## Change or remove Income

Open the line's actions on the Income page.

- **Edit income** changes it. If you typed it in, everything about it can be changed. If it came from your bank, its amount and date stay as the bank has them.
- **It's between us · not Income** marks money the other Parent sent you.
- **Remove income** takes it away.

## Pay that varies

When a Parent's pay varies, the Income page shows its range over the last three full months, like "$1,800 so far · usually $2,100–$2,900". The Plan still counts on the low end. When the low end moves, Noodle offers **Use $2,300 as what you can count on**, with the new figure.

## Good to know

- Changing the kind never changes the amount or the date.
- A Refund or money Paid back gives its Bucket or Commitment the money back. It does not add to Income.
- Money in that waits to be looked at counts nowhere until you say what it is.
