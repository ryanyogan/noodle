One trip to a big store can cover groceries, clothes and a birthday present. A Receipt lists what was bought, item by item. Give Noodle the Receipt and it attaches it to the Transaction and works out the Splits: how much of the total belongs in each Bucket.

There are two ways to give Noodle a Receipt: forward an email, or snap a photo.

## Forward a receipt email

Your Household has one Receipt address. You set it up once.

1. Open **Household settings** and find **Forward receipts**.
2. Press **Get an address**.
3. Keep the address somewhere handy, for example in your email contacts.
4. When a store emails you a receipt, forward it to that address from your own email.

Noodle attaches the Receipt to its Transaction. If there is no Transaction for it yet, Noodle makes a Quick Add.

> Only the Household's Parents can forward to the address, and only from an email address they have verified.

## Snap a paper Receipt

Open Quick Add and tap **Snap receipt**. Noodle reads the photo and fills in Quick Add for you to check. Nothing is saved until you tap a Bucket. See [Quick Add](/docs/quick-add).

## Splits from a Receipt

When Noodle is sure of every item, and nobody has already decided where the Transaction goes, it applies the Splits by itself. The Transaction then says it was filed from its Receipt.

Otherwise you apply them:

1. Open the Transaction on the Transactions page.
2. Look at the Receipt and the Splits it proposes.
3. Choose a Bucket for any item that does not have one.
4. Press **Apply these Splits**.

Each discount stays with its item, and tax is shared out across the items, so the Splits add up to the total.

## When a Receipt cannot be used

Noodle tells you on the Transaction when it could not propose Splits.

- Its total could not be read.
- Its lines do not add up to its total.
- Its total is not the Transaction's amount.

The Receipt stays attached. You can still split the Transaction by hand with **Add Split**.

## Make a new address

If the Receipt address ends up somewhere it should not be, press **Make a new address** in Household settings. The old one stops working, and mail sent to it bounces. Anything already forwarded stays.

## Good to know

- You do not need a Receipt for every purchase. They help most when one purchase covers more than one Bucket.
- A Split has its own amount, its own Bucket, and its own For, so one Receipt can cover spending for different Members.
