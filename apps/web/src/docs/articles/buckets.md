A Bucket is a monthly allowance for one kind of day-to-day spending, like Groceries, Fuel or Fun. Every purchase is filed in a Bucket, and the Bucket shows how much is left. That one number, what is left, is what lets you say yes or no at the shop without doing sums.

## Where to find it

- **This Month** shows every Bucket and what is left in it.
- **Plan**, on the Overview page under **Buckets**, is where you add and change them.

Choose a Bucket in either place to open it.

## Add Buckets

1. Open **Plan** and go to **Buckets**.
2. Choose **Add Buckets**.
3. Tick the starter Buckets you want, and set what each gets a month.
4. For one of your own, choose **Add your own** and name it.
5. Tick **Carries over** for any Bucket that should keep its leftover.
6. Choose **Add Buckets**.

If Noodle has read your spending, some amounts are marked "Suggested from your spending". Change them as you like.

## Change a Bucket

1. Open the Bucket and choose **Edit Bucket**.
2. Change its allowance, its name, or its group.
3. Under **At the end of the month**, choose Resets monthly or Carries over.
4. Save.

While you type an allowance, the sheet shows **Free to Spend after this**, so you see the effect before saving. A changed allowance reaches that month and later months, or just that month, as you choose.

## Resets monthly or Carries over

- **Resets monthly.** The Bucket starts each month at its allowance. What is left when the month ends can go to a Goal. New Buckets work this way.
- **Carries over.** What is left carries into next month, and so does overspending that is not Covered. Good for spending that comes in bursts, like gifts, clothes or car repairs.

## Reading a Bucket

- **Planned** is the allowance for the month.
- **Available** is what the Bucket has to spend this month: the allowance, plus what carried over, plus or minus anything Moved in or out.
- **Left** is Available less what has been spent.
- **Pace** is where spending would be by today if you spent evenly across the month. The line on the bar marks it. A Bucket shows **On pace** or **Ahead of pace**. Ahead means it is being spent faster than the month is going.

A Bucket's own page also shows its Transactions for the month, how spending compared with the allowance over recent months, and its allowance history.

## Order and groups

Drag a Bucket to move it in the list, or use **Move up** and **Move down** in its edit sheet. Put Buckets in a group by typing a group name there. Buckets in a group are listed together, with a subtotal.

## Archive, restore or delete

- **Archive** takes a Bucket out of the Plan from that month on. Earlier months keep it, with everything filed in it.
- **Restore to the Plan**, on an archived Bucket's page, brings it back.
- A Bucket can be **deleted** only if nothing ever touched it: nothing filed in it, no money Moved in or out, no Rule, and no earlier month's Plan. Otherwise archive it.

## Suggested Buckets

When Noodle spots regular spending with no Bucket of its own, it offers one under the list on the Plan, with what it is based on: "About $120 a month across 9 charges". Choose **Add Bucket**, or **Not now** to put it away.

## Good to know

- Start with a handful. Too many Buckets makes filing a chore.
- When a Bucket goes over, Cover it from another Bucket or from Free to Spend. See [When you overspend](/docs/when-you-overspend).
- A Refund goes back into the Bucket the purchase came from.
- You can ask for a Nudge when a Bucket is being spent faster than the month is going.
- A Personal Allowance is a Bucket of one Parent's own. See [Personal Allowance](/docs/personal-allowance).
