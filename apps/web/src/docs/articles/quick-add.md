Quick Add is how you enter a Transaction by hand, right when you pay. Type the amount, tap a Bucket, done. Your Bucket shows what is left straight away, without waiting for the bank.

## Where to find it

On a phone, Quick Add is the round button in the middle of the bottom bar. On a computer it is the button at the top of the sidebar, and the Q key opens it too. It opens over whatever page you are on.

## Add what you spent

1. Open Quick Add.
2. Type the amount.
3. Add a note if you like, under "Where or what?"
4. Tap the Bucket it belongs to. That saves it.

Quick Add offers the five Buckets you are most likely to want. If the note names a merchant you have a Rule for, or have filed before, that Bucket comes first. Then come Buckets you have used recently around this time of day.

For any other Bucket, tap **More Buckets** and search. On a computer, use **Find a Bucket**: type a few letters, move with the arrow keys, and press Enter.

## Snap a Receipt

1. In Quick Add, tap **Snap receipt** and take a photo of a paper Receipt.
2. Noodle reads it and fills in what it can.
3. Check it, then tap a Bucket to add it.

A Quick Add saved from a snapped Receipt is dated as the Receipt is, and the Receipt is attached to it. You can split it across Buckets afterwards from Transactions.

## Say it

1. In Quick Add, tap **Say it**.
2. Say what you spent, for example "forty on pizza after hockey".
3. Noodle fills in the amount, a suggested Bucket and a note. Check it, then tap a Bucket.

Nothing is saved until you tap a Bucket. If Noodle cannot read a photo or what you said, it tells you, and you can type the amount.

## Tap to capture on an iPhone

An iPhone Shortcut can add a Quick Add for you each time you pay with Wallet, without opening Noodle.

1. Open **Household settings** and find **Tap to capture**.
2. Press **Set up tap to capture**. Noodle shows a URL and a token to put in the Shortcut.
3. Copy the token then. It is shown only once.

A purchase captured this way is filed the way bank Transactions are. If Noodle is not sure, it goes to Review. **Revoke token** stops the Shortcut adding anything. Anyone who has your token can add Quick Adds as you, so keep it to yourself.

## When the bank catches up

If the Account also gets Transactions from a bank or a statement, the same purchase will arrive again later. Noodle Matches the bank's line with your Quick Add so it counts once. Until then, your Quick Add shows **Waiting for bank** on Transactions.

## Good to know

- Quick Add files spending into a [Bucket](/docs/buckets), so your Plan needs at least one Bucket first.
- You can file in your own [Personal Allowance](/docs/personal-allowance). The other Parent's is never offered.
- Made a mistake? Open it on Transactions to change or delete it.
