This Month answers "where are we now?" Reports answer "where did it go?" They add up your Transactions over a period you choose and show them from ten angles. Every chart has a table of the same numbers, and most figures open to show what is behind them.

## Where to find it

On a computer, Reports is in the sidebar under Understand. On a phone, open **More** and choose Reports.

Reports fill in once you have Transactions. Until then the page says so, and offers Quick Add and connecting a bank.

## The ten views

The tabs across the top are the views.

- **Overview.** The headlines for the period: Spent, Earned, Saved and Free to Spend.
- **Big expenses.** The largest costs in the period.
- **Buckets.** Spending by Bucket.
- **Plan vs actual.** What was planned for each Bucket against what was spent.
- **Trends.** Spending over time.
- **Merchants.** Where you spend.
- **People.** Spending For each person in the Household.
- **Cash flow.** Money in against money out.
- **Goals.** How each Goal is coming along against its target.
- **Income.** What was received, beside your Take-home pay and Extra income.

## Choose the period

**Period** sets the stretch of time every view covers.

- This month
- Last month
- Last 3 months
- Last 6 months
- Last 12 months
- Year to date
- Last year
- Custom range, with a **From** and a **To** date

## Compare and group

Some views offer two more choices. A view shows only the choices it uses.

- **Compare with** sets the period beside an earlier one: **vs previous**, **vs last year**, or **No comparison**. If your history does not go back far enough, Reports say so.
- **Group by** sets the size of each step in a chart: **By week**, **By month** or **By quarter**. Left alone, Reports pick one to suit the period and mark it "(auto)".

## Filters

**Filters** narrows every view to some of your spending.

1. Press **Filters**.
2. Choose any of: **Buckets**, **For** (Anyone, The whole Household, or one Member), **Account**, **Merchant**, and **At least**, a smallest amount in whole dollars.
3. Press **Apply**.

Each filter you set shows as a small chip. Take a chip off to remove that filter, or use **Clear all** in the sheet.

On a phone, Period, Compare with and Group by are in the Filters sheet too.

## See what is behind a figure

Where a row or a mark in a chart can be opened, press it to see what it stands for: one Bucket, one merchant or one person. On a phone, the first tap on a chart shows the figure and the second opens it.

Inside, you see that one thing over the period, with its Transactions and a link to **Open in Transactions**. You can go one step further, to a single month of it.

A trail above the view shows where you are, for example "Reports › Buckets › Groceries › October 2026". Press any step in it to go back up.

## Charts and tables

Each chart has a button to show it as a table. The table holds the same numbers as the chart.

**Export CSV**, at the top of the page, downloads the tables of the view you are looking at, for the period and filters you have set. Open the file in any spreadsheet.

## Good to know

- Every choice is kept in the page's address. Bookmark a view, or send the link to the other Parent, and it opens the same way.
- The other Parent's Personal Allowance shows as a total with a lock. You cannot open it. Your own opens like any Bucket.
- Reports only read. Nothing you do here changes your Plan or your Transactions.
