Every family overspends a Bucket sometimes. A birthday, a car repair, a week of takeaway. A Plan is a best guess made before the month happened, so going over is not failing. What matters is deciding where the money comes from.

## The idea

When one Bucket goes over, the money has already left your bank account. The only question is which other part of the Plan gives something up to pay for it.

If you decide, you stay in charge: less Fun this month because the car needed brakes. If you do not decide, the Plan decides for you, and usually it is next month that pays.

## Cover it

A **Cover** brings an overspent Bucket back to $0 with money from another Bucket or from Free to Spend, in the same month. Nothing moves at your bank. You are changing the Plan to match the month you are having.

1. Open **This Month**. Overspent Buckets are listed first.
2. Choose **Cover** on the Bucket that is over.
3. Under **Cover from**, pick where the money comes from: a Bucket with some left, or Free to Spend.
4. Enter an amount, up to what the Bucket is over, and save.

The Bucket you took from now has less left for the rest of the month.

> You can Cover in the month you are in. A month that has ended can no longer be Covered.

## If nothing has money left

Sometimes every Bucket is spent and Free to Spend is at zero. The Cover sheet says so, and offers **Edit Plan**. Raising the Bucket's allowance in the Plan brings it back to zero, but it takes Free to Spend below zero. That is the Plan telling you the month costs more than came in.

## If you do not Cover it

What happens next depends on how the Bucket works.

- **Carries over.** The overspending comes out of next month. If Clothes is $40 over, next month's Clothes starts $40 lower.
- **Resets monthly.** The Bucket starts next month at its full allowance again. The overspending still happened, so the month ends with less, and less is carried over into next month's Free to Spend.

Either way the money is accounted for. Covering just lets you choose where it comes from.

## When Free to Spend is below zero

Free to Spend below zero means the Plan promises more than comes in. To bring it back above zero you can:

- lower a Bucket's allowance,
- end a Commitment you no longer need,
- fund a Goal a little less this month.

A month that ends short is carried over too. Next month's Free to Spend starts lower by that amount, and This Month shows it, for example "$230 short carried over from September".

## When the same Bucket keeps going over

One bad month is life. The same Bucket over most months means its allowance is too low for how your family really lives.

The Plan lists this under **Things to check**: "Groceries is over its allowance most months". Raise the allowance, and lower something else to make room. A Plan that matches real life is easier to keep than one that looks good on paper.

## Good to know

- A Cover is a Move: planned money going from one place in the Plan to another. It is not a Transfer, which is real money moving between your Accounts.
- Only you can Cover your own Personal Allowance, or Cover from it.
- A Cover can be undone.
- A Refund goes back into the Bucket the purchase came from, so returning something can fix an overspent Bucket by itself.
