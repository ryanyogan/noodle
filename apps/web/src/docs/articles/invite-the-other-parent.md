A Household has two Parents who share one Plan. Once the other Parent joins, you both see the same numbers and either of you can change them. This page shows how to invite them and what they see when they arrive.

## Where to find it

Setup asks you to invite the other Parent as one of its steps. You can also do it any time on **Household settings**, under People, in **Invite the other Parent**. On a computer, Household settings is in the sidebar. On a phone, it is under More.

## Send the invite

1. Type their email in **Their email**.
2. Choose **Invite**.
3. Noodle emails them a link to join your Household. The link works for 7 days.

Right after you invite, Noodle also shows the invite link with **Copy link**. Copy it and send it yourself if you would rather text it, or if the email could not be sent.

> The link is shown only once. If you lose it, choose Resend to get a new one.

## While you wait

Household settings shows who you invited, when the invite was sent and when it runs out, with a **Waiting** badge.

- **Resend** sends a new link. The old link then stops working. You can resend after a minute, and a Household can send five invite emails a day.
- **Cancel invite** stops the link working. You can invite them again any time.
- **Invite someone else** replaces the invite if you typed the wrong email.

If the invite runs out, it shows **Ran out**. Resend it for a new link that works for another 7 days.

## What the other Parent does

1. They open the link in the email and sign up.
2. On **Join** with your Household's name, they type their name and join.
3. They see **Here's your Household**: the Parents, the Take-home pay, and how many bills, Buckets and Goals are set up so far.
4. They can add their own Personal Allowance there, or leave it for later.
5. **Go to This Month** takes them into Noodle.

They do not go through Setup again. What you set up is already theirs too.

They can also join without the link, by signing in to Noodle with the email you invited.

## What you both share

- One Plan: the same Take-home pay, Commitments, Buckets and Goals.
- The same Accounts and Transactions.
- Either Parent can change the Plan. **What changed** on the Plan lists the changes.

The one private thing is each Parent's Personal Allowance. The other Parent sees its totals, never what was bought. See [Personal Allowance](/docs/personal-allowance).

## Good to know

- An invite link works once. If someone has already joined with it, it cannot be used again.
- A Household has two Parents. Once both have joined, no more invites can be sent.
- A person can belong to one Household only.
- The invite email never includes amounts. It names you and your Household, and nothing else about your money.
