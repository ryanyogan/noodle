Household settings is the place for everything about your Household that is not money: who is in it, when Noodle reminds you, and how to take your data out or clear it. It has three tabs: **Settings**, **Logs** and **Changelog**.

## Where to find it

**Household settings** is in the sidebar and in the account menu on a computer, and in More on a phone.

## Settings

The first tab, and where Household settings opens.

- **Household.** The Household name and its time zone. The time zone decides when a new month starts.
- **People.** The Parents, with your own name and colour. An invite for the other Parent, if they have not joined. Your Children.
- **Bringing in spending.** The address for forwarding Receipts, and Tap to capture, which adds a Quick Add when you pay with Wallet on an iPhone.
- **Setup.** **Run setup again** walks you through the Setup steps once more. It changes what is there. Nothing is added twice.
- **Reminders.** Your Check-in day, and Nudges.
- **Your data.** Download your data, and Snapshots.
- **Danger zone.** Start fresh and Delete Household.

## Logs

The **Logs** tab holds the Log: every change to the Plan in one table, newest first.

- Each row says when (the day and the time), who, what, the change and the month it takes effect.
- It also lists Rules made and removed (Rules for money in and remembered card payments too), snapshots, Fresh starts, Bank Connections connected and disconnected, and Accounts archived.
- A Rule you remove stays listed twice: when it was made, and when it was removed.
- Narrow it to one Parent or one kind of item. On a phone, **Sort** puts it Newest, Oldest or by Who.
- "See what changed" on the Plan opens it at that month.
- After a Fresh start the Log keeps the Fresh start, your snapshots and what had been removed before it. The Plan changes, Rules and Bank Connections it cleared are no longer listed.
- The other Parent's Personal Allowance only ever shows as "Personal Allowance changed".

## Changelog

The **Changelog** tab lists what changed in Noodle itself, release by release, newest first. Each release has its date, a short title and its changes. Press a release's title to get an address that opens the Changelog at that release.

## Turn on Nudges

Nudges are set for each device.

1. Open **Household settings** on the device you want Nudges on. It opens on the Settings tab.
2. Under Reminders, press **Turn on**. Your device may ask you to allow notifications.
3. Choose which Nudges you get: a Bucket being spent faster than the month is going, the other Parent's Quick Adds, and Extra income arriving.
4. Set **Quiet hours** if you want Nudges to wait.
5. Press **Send a test** to check it works.

On an iPhone or iPad, add Noodle to your Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and open it from there.

## Start fresh

Start fresh empties your Household so you can begin again.

- It clears every Transaction, Account, bank connection, Bucket, Commitment, Goal, Rule and Insight, every month's Plan and its changes, and Noodle's notes on your spending.
- It keeps your Household, its Parents and its Children.
- Noodle takes a snapshot first. For up to 90 days you can put your Household back from Snapshots, statement and Receipt files included.
- Two things do not come back with a snapshot. Banks need connecting again. A data download left in Noodle is cleared for good.

1. On the Settings tab, under Danger zone, press **Start fresh**.
2. Read the list of what goes. To keep a copy, press **Download everything first** and save the file to your phone or computer.
3. Press **Continue**.
4. Type your Household's name. If banks are connected, tick the box that reads **Disconnect**, the bank's name, **from Noodle**. Noodle tells you what is still needed.
5. Press **Start fresh**.

### When it happens

- **With two Parents**, it does not happen at once. It is set for 24 hours later, and the other Parent is told straight away. Until then, Household settings shows when it will happen, and either of you can press **Cancel**.
- The other Parent can agree sooner. They press **Start it now** and type the Household's name, and it happens as soon as they confirm.
- **With one Parent**, it happens as soon as you confirm.
- Once it starts, it cannot be stopped.
- When it has finished, Noodle says your Household is empty and offers **Set up your Household**.
- If it stops partway, Noodle says what is done so far and offers **Try again**, which carries on from that step.

## Delete Household

> Deleting cannot be undone. Start fresh can be put back. Deleting cannot.

Delete Household removes everything Start fresh clears, and also the Household itself, its Parents and Children, and every snapshot in Snapshots. A data download left in Noodle is deleted too.

Noodle keeps one last snapshot for 30 days, then deletes it. You cannot put it back yourself, so do not count on it. On the last step you can tick **Also delete the last snapshot**, and then nothing is kept.

1. On the Settings tab, under Danger zone, press **Delete Household**.
2. Read the list of what goes. Press **Download everything first** if you want a copy, and save it to your phone or computer.
3. Press **Continue**.
4. Type your Household's name. If banks are connected, tick the box to disconnect them.
5. Press **Delete Household**.

The timing is the same as Start fresh: 24 hours later with two Parents, with **Cancel** for either of you until then, and at once with one Parent. Once deleting starts, it cannot be stopped or undone.
