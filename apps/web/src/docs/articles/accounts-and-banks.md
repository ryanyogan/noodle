An Account is a real place your money lives or is owed: checking, savings, a credit card, a line of credit or a loan. Every Transaction comes from one. The Accounts page lists them and lets you choose how Noodle knows about each.

## Where to find it

On a computer, Accounts is in the sidebar under Day to day. On a phone, open **More** and choose Accounts.

The page groups your Accounts as **Cash** and **Cards and loans**. **Totals** shows what they add up to, and how much of your cash is Set aside for [Goals](/docs/goals) or Not set aside.

## Three ways to add an Account

- **Connect your bank.** Noodle brings in balances and Transactions for you.
- **Upload statements.** Add the Account, then upload statement files on its page. See [Uploading a statement](/docs/upload-a-statement).
- **Type in a balance.** For cash, or a bank Noodle cannot reach. You update the balance yourself, and your Quick Adds are its spending.

Press **Add Account** for the second and third.

## Connect a bank

1. On Accounts, press **Connect a bank**.
2. Choose how far back Noodle should bring in what you spent. This month only is a fresh start. You can also choose the last 30, 60, 90, 120 or 365 days.
3. Press **Continue to your bank** and log in there.
4. Noodle lists the accounts at that bank and asks "Which of these do you have already?" For each one, choose **Same as** the Account it already is, **Add as a new Account**, or **Leave it out**.
5. Press **Start bringing them in**.

> How far back cannot be changed later for that connection. Older spending stays at your bank.

Noodle reads balances and Transactions. It cannot move money.

Pairing a bank account with an Account you already have keeps its Goals and history. Lines a statement already brought in are not added twice.

## Reconnect

Sometimes a bank wants you to log in again. The connection then says so, and its Accounts show **Log in again**. Nothing new comes in until you do.

1. Open Accounts and find the bank under **Bank Connections**.
2. Press **Reconnect** and log in at the bank.

Reconnecting keeps the same connection, its Accounts and everything on them.

## Stop syncing or disconnect

- **Stop syncing one Account.** Open the Account and, under **More**, press **Stop syncing with** your bank. Its Transactions stay. Nothing new comes in for it. Your other Accounts at that bank keep syncing.
- **Disconnect the whole bank.** Choose **Disconnect** for that bank. Its Accounts stay, with their Transactions and statements, and are kept by hand from then on.

If you stop syncing the only Account at a bank, the bank is disconnected too. After either one, add new spending with Quick Add or by uploading a statement.

## Archive an Account

Archive an Account you no longer use. It leaves the Accounts list, the pickers and the totals. Its Transactions stay, so past months do not change.

1. Open the Account.
2. Under **More**, press **Archive this Account** and confirm.

To bring it back, open **Archived** at the bottom of Accounts and press **Restore**.

## Good to know

- An Account with a Goal kept in it cannot be archived until the Goal is archived.
- An Account a Commitment pays down cannot be archived until you end that Commitment or change what it pays down.
- On an Account you keep by hand, use **Update balance**, or **Update what's owed** for a card or loan.
- An Account with no balance yet is not counted in Totals, and the page tells you.
- On a card or loan, **Plan to pay this off** starts a Goal to bring what is owed down to $0. See [Paying off debt](/docs/paying-off-debt).
- Open any Account to rename it and to see its latest Transactions.
