Noodle files most spending in its Bucket or Commitment by itself. When it is not sure, the Transaction waits in Review. You confirm Noodle's suggestion or pick somewhere else. A Rule then lets Noodle file that merchant on its own next time.

## Where to find it

Press **Review** at the top of the Transactions page. It shows how many are waiting. On a computer, the same count sits beside Transactions in the sidebar. Review has two tabs: **Review** and **Rules**.

## Work through the cards

Review shows one card at a time. Each card says what the Transaction was, where Noodle suggests filing it, and why: your Rule, something similar you filed before, or Noodle's own suggestion.

1. Press **Confirm** to file it where Noodle suggests. On a phone, swipe right.
2. To file it somewhere else, open the picker on the card and choose a Bucket or Commitment. On a phone, swipe left.
3. Press **Skip** to send a card to the back and decide later.
4. Press **Undo** to put the last card back.

Under the card are three more choices: **Split** it across Buckets, file it in your own **Personal Allowance**, or **Make a Rule**.

On a computer the keys work too: right arrow or Enter confirms, left arrow picks another, down arrow skips.

## See them as a list

Switch from **One by one** to **List** to see every card at once. In the list you can use **Confirm all** to file everything where Noodle suggested, or confirm all the cards from one merchant together. A card's pencil opens it, to split it, change its note or say who it was For.

**Look again** asks Noodle to go over what is waiting once more.

## "Always file…" offers

After you file a card, Noodle may ask "Always file" that merchant in the Bucket you chose. Press **Always file** and it becomes a Rule. If the Bucket is your Personal Allowance, only you will see that Rule.

## Card payments and money between you

Some cards are not spending at all, and Review says so.

- **It's a card payment.** A payment to a card Noodle follows is a Transfer. What you bought on the card is already in your Buckets. Mark it and it leaves Review without counting anywhere.
- **A card Noodle does not follow.** Noodle cannot see what was bought on it, so the payment is the spending. You can **Make it a Commitment**, or connect the card.
- **It's between us.** Money one Parent sent the other is not spending. If it paid someone else, pick a Bucket.

## File without a Bucket

A Transaction from a month before your Plan started, or from a month with no Buckets, has nowhere to go. **File without a Bucket** takes it out of Review and changes no month's figures.

## The Rules page

The **Rules** tab lists your Rules. Each shows the words it looks for, where it files, who it is For, and how many it has filed so far.

1. Press **Add Rule**.
2. Type a word from the merchant's name.
3. Choose where it files: a Bucket or a Commitment.
4. Save it. You can also file what matches right away.

Open a Rule to change it, to **File what's still unassigned now**, or to **Delete Rule**. Deleting a Rule leaves what it already filed where it is.

## Good to know

- A Rule looks at the bank's own wording, so renaming a Transaction never unfiles anything.
- A Rule into your Personal Allowance is marked **Only you**. The other Parent never sees it.
- A card you put back with Undo waits for you. Noodle does not file it again by itself.
- When nothing is waiting, Review says "Nothing to review" and how many Noodle filed on its own this month.
