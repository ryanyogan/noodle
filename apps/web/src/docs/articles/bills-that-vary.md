Some bills come every month but never for the same amount: power, water, gas. Plan them as a Commitment whose amount is **about** what it usually is.

## Set a bill to "about"

1. Open **Plan**, then **Commitments**, and open the bill.
2. Choose **Edit Commitment**. On a computer the button reads **Edit**.
3. Change the amount from **The same each time** to **About: it varies**, and save.

## What "about" means

The amount is the average of the bill's charges over the last year. Until there is a year of them, it is the average of the last three. Noodle shows it with how far the charges range, like "About $160 · $120–$210".

## When a charge comes in over or under

The real charge is what counts. This Month says where the difference goes:

- "Power came in $35 over · comes out of what carries to November"
- "Power came in $20.50 under · adds to what carries to January"

Free to Spend does not change in the middle of the month because the power bill was high.

## Good to know

- Noodle does not suggest a new amount each time an "about" bill moves. It suggests one when the average has drifted by more than a tenth.
- The bill's terms history says when it became "about".
- A bill that is the same every time, like the mortgage, should stay **The same each time**.
