Sometimes one purchase belongs in more than one place. A big shop might be mostly Groceries, with some hockey tape and a birthday present. A **Split** is one part of a Transaction, with its own amount, its own Bucket or Commitment, and its own For.

## Where to find it

- On **Transactions**, open the Transaction. Its editor has a **Split** button.
- In **Review**, each card has a **Split** button.

## Split by hand

1. Open the Transaction and press **Split**.
2. For each Split, enter an **Amount** and choose what it is **Assigned to**: a Bucket or a Commitment.
3. Set **For** on each Split if the parts were for different people.
4. Press **Add Split** if you need another part.
5. Save. The Splits must add up to the Transaction's amount, and Noodle tells you if they do not.

To go back to one piece, press **Remove Splits**.

## Split from a Receipt

If a Receipt is attached to the Transaction, Noodle reads its items and proposes Splits. Each discount stays with its item, and tax is shared out.

1. Forward the Receipt to your Household's Receipt address, or snap it in Quick Add.
2. Open the Transaction. The proposed Splits are listed under the Receipt.
3. Choose a Bucket for each item that does not have one. Noodle will not apply the Splits until every item has one.
4. Press **Apply these Splits**.

When Noodle is sure of every item and nobody has filed the Transaction yet, it applies the Splits by itself. The Transaction then reads "Split from its Receipt."

## Good to know

- Each Split counts in its own Bucket, so every Bucket's "left" stays true.
- If a Receipt's lines do not add up to its total, Noodle proposes no Splits. You can still split by hand.
- A Transaction with part of it in the other Parent's Personal Allowance can only be changed by that Parent.
- On a computer, pressing S on a Review card starts a Split.
