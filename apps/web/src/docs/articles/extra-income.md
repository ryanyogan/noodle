Extra income is money that comes in above your Take-home pay: a bigger paycheck, a third paycheck, a bonus, a tax refund. Noodle never spends it or saves it for you. It waits until a Parent decides.

## Where to find it

When there is Extra income, This Month shows an **Extra income** section with the amount. The Check-in has a step for it, and so does closing a month.

Income from a connected bank or a statement arrives by itself. To record income by hand, use **Add income** in the Income section of This Month.

## Deciding where it goes

You have three choices: Free to Spend, a Goal, or a Bucket.

1. Open **This Month** and find **Extra income**.
2. To use it this month, press **Add** the amount **to Free to Spend**.
3. To send it somewhere else, press **Choose where**.
4. Under **To**, pick Free to Spend, a Goal or a Bucket.
5. Check the **Amount**. You can send part of it and decide the rest later.
6. Press **Send**.

Noodle may list a few suggestions under the buttons, such as a Bucket that is overspent, your emergency Goal, or a Goal that is behind schedule. Each has its own **Send** button. They are only suggestions.

## When a month has ended

If Extra income is still undecided when the month ends, it shows up when you close that month. There the choices are a Goal, **Leave it in the account**, or **Decide later**.

## When less comes in than usual

In a low month, Noodle offers a button that reads **Lower take-home pay to** and the amount that arrived. It lowers Take-home pay for just that month, and Free to Spend goes down by the same amount. Later months do not change. Noodle never does this by itself, and you can undo it straight away.

## Good to know

- A few dollars over is not Extra income. Up to $25 above your Take-home pay is treated as your usual pay landing slightly different.
- If someone's pay varies, set Take-home pay to the amount you can count on. Better months then show up as Extra income.
- You can ask for a Nudge when Extra income arrives. Turn it on in Household settings, under Reminders.
- A Refund of a purchase is not income. It goes back to the Bucket the purchase came from.
- Money the other Parent sent you is not income either. See [Money between Parents](/docs/money-between-parents).
