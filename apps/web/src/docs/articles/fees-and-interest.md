A late fee, a monthly account fee, interest on a card: none of it buys anything, and it is easy to lose among the rest. Noodle files it in a Bucket named **Fees and interest**, so the month shows what it cost.

## How it gets filed

When a charge in Review reads as a fee or as interest, the card says "Looks like a fee from your bank or card" or "Looks like interest you were charged", and suggests the **Fees and interest** Bucket.

Confirm it, and Noodle:

- adds the Bucket to your Plan if it is not there yet (the card says so),
- files the charge in it,
- makes a Rule, so the next one files itself.

You can still pick any other Bucket on the card.

## The Bucket

Fees and interest is an ordinary Bucket. It starts with no allowance and resets each month. Give it an allowance if you expect fees, or leave it at $0 so that every fee shows as over.

## Good to know

- Interest you earn is Income, not a fee.
- Noodle only suggests. Nothing is filed there until you confirm, or a Rule you made does it.
- A month that is over is not changed: the Bucket is only added to a Plan that is still open.
