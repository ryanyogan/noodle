Credit cards, phone plans and memberships often include services or pay back certain costs. It is easy to forget them and pay for the same thing twice. Perks & Benefits lists what you already hold and what comes with it.

Noodle uses two words here. A **Perk Source** is the product you hold: a credit card, a phone plan, a membership or an insurance policy. A **Perk** is one benefit it includes: a service, a cost it pays back, or a kind of purchase it earns more on.

## Where to find it

On a computer, Perks & Benefits is in the sidebar under Understand. On a phone, open **More**. It is also the **Perks** tab on Insights. A credit card's own page on Accounts links to its Perks too.

## Add a Perk Source

A credit card that a connected bank brings in becomes a Perk Source by itself. If the bank does not say which card it is, Noodle asks you once.

To add anything else:

1. Press **Add a card or membership**.
2. Search for it, or type its name.
3. If Noodle does not know it, that is fine. Noodle looks for its benefits page, and asks you for the address if it cannot find one.

Noodle also suggests Perk Sources it spots in your spending. They wait under **To confirm**. Press **Confirm** if you hold it, or **Not ours** if you do not.

## See what is worth using

**Worth using now** sets your Perks against your own spending. It points out things like a credit you have not used yet this period, with the day it resets.

When you use a Perk:

1. Find it under its Perk Source.
2. Press **Mark used**. You can add a note.
3. Changed your mind? Press **Undo**.

The page adds up the value you have used this year and sets it against annual fees, so you can see whether a card is earning its keep.

## Where each Perk comes from

Noodle reads Perks only from the provider's own benefits page. Open **Where this comes from** on a Perk to see the link it came from, the page's own words, and the date it was last checked.

- If a Perk has no value yet, use **Add its value**.
- If something looks out of date, use **Check again**.
- To stop tracking a Perk Source, use **Remove**. Its Perks, and the Insights resting on them, go too, and Noodle will not suggest it again.

## Good to know

- Perks are only ever about something your Household already has. Noodle does not suggest new cards.
- A Perk that covers something you also pay for separately shows up as an Overlap in [Insights](/docs/insights).
- Benefits change. Check the provider's page, linked from each Perk, before you rely on one.
- You can set a Perk Source's annual fee and say which card it is at any time.
