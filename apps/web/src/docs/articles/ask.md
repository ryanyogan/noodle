Ask lets you type a question about your Household's money the way you would say it. The answer comes from your real figures, and it lists the figures it used, with links to the pages that show more.

## Where to find it

On a computer, Ask is the small button in the corner of every page. On a phone, open **More** and choose Ask.

## Ask a question

1. Open Ask.
2. Type your question, or tap one of the examples.
3. Read the answer. Under it, **Figures** lists the numbers the answer used.
4. Follow a link to see more, for example **See Transactions**, **Open Goals** or **Open in Explore**.

Some questions to try:

- How are we doing this month?
- How much have we spent this year?
- Are our Goals on track?
- Can we afford a $2,000 trip?

## What Ask can answer

Ask can look up five kinds of things.

- **A month of your Plan.** Take-home pay, Free to Spend, and what is left in each Bucket.
- **Spending over a range of months.** In one Bucket or Commitment, or across all Buckets, and For one Member if you name them.
- **Your Goals.** What each has, what is left to save, and whether it is on track.
- **Whether you can afford something.** Give it a price and it runs an Affordability Check: Comfortable, Stretch or Not yet.
- **A simple "what if".** What changing one Bucket's allowance, or ending or changing one Commitment, would free up over the next year.

## What Ask cannot do

- It does not change anything. It only reads.
- It answers about your Household's own figures in Noodle. It is not a source of general financial advice.
- It does not remember. Each visit starts fresh.
- For anything outside the five kinds above, a page like Reports or Transactions will serve you better.

If Ask cannot answer, it says so and offers a retry. Rewording the question to name a Bucket, a month or a price often helps.

## Good to know

- Questions are not saved. They are gone when you leave the page.
- Ask sees what you may see. The other Parent's Personal Allowance is totals only.
- Check the figures. Ask shows the numbers it used so you can see where an answer came from.
- Not sure what a word means? Ask links to the [Glossary](/glossary).
