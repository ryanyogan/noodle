Explore is for "what if?" What if you ended a subscription, spent less eating out, or took on a car payment? Explore shows how a change plays out over the coming months without touching your real Plan.

## Where to find it

On a computer, Explore is in the sidebar under Planning. On a phone, open **More** and choose Explore. It has three tabs: **Explore**, **Can we afford it?** and **Scenarios**.

You need a Plan first. Without Take-home pay and something planned, there is nothing to try changes on.

## Try a change

A **Scenario** is a copy of your Plan with changes made to it. Explore starts with the Plan as it stands.

1. Open **Explore**.
2. Change something: your Take-home pay, a Commitment, a Bucket's allowance or a Goal. You can also end a Commitment, add a new one, or add a one-off expense or income.
3. Look at **How it plays out**. It sets the Plan against your Scenario: Free to Spend each month, your Goals, and any warnings the change causes or clears.
4. Give the Scenario a name to save it.

You can add an assumption for raises and inflation, and switch a change off for a moment to see the outcome without it.

## Projected balance

The **Projected balance** is what would be left month by month if you spend what is planned. Its lowest point, and the first month it goes below zero, tell you whether a Scenario holds up.

## Compare saved Scenarios

The **Scenarios** tab lists every saved Scenario, with who made it and whether it was applied. Tick up to three to compare them side by side, as charts and key numbers, each against the Plan.

## Make it real

1. Open the Scenario and press **Apply to the Plan**.
2. Noodle shows exactly what will change in the Plan.
3. Confirm.

Only real Plan amounts are applied. A one-off expense and the raises and inflation assumption are not, and neither is a change you switched off. A one-off expense can be made a Goal instead.

## Can we afford it?

The **Affordability Check** answers one question about a specific purchase: a home, a car, or anything else with a price.

1. Open **Can we afford it?**
2. Choose what to check and fill in the costs. For a home that means things like price, down payment, rate and property tax.
3. Read the answer: **Comfortable**, **Stretch** or **Not yet**.

Under the answer, **Reasons** says why, and **How this is worked out** shows the sums. The Check weighs the cost against your Take-home pay, your Free to Spend in a typical month, and what your Goals have Set aside.

From a Check you can press **Explore as a Scenario** to see the monthly costs play out, or turn the purchase into a Goal.

## Good to know

- Nothing in Explore changes your Plan until you apply a Scenario.
- The Affordability Check is a guide. It assumes no interest on savings, no inflation and no raises. It is not a lender's decision or financial advice.
- "Try in Explore" on an Insight, and "Try ending this" on a Commitment, open Explore with the change already made.
- You never see or change the other Parent's Personal Allowance purchases here.
