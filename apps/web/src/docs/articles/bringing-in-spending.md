A Plan is only useful when Noodle knows what you really spent. There are three ways to tell it. You can mix them, one Account at a time, and you can change your mind later.

## The three ways

### Connect a bank

You log in to your bank in its own window. Noodle then reads your balances and your spending, and brings in new spending every day. It files most of it for you, and you check the few it is not sure of in Review.

Noodle only reads. It cannot move money.

### Upload a statement

You download a statement file from your bank's website and upload it to Noodle now and then. Noodle reads CSV, OFX and QFX files. Uploading the same statement twice, or two that overlap, adds nothing twice.

### Type it in

You add each purchase with Quick Add at the moment you pay: type the amount and tap a Bucket. Nothing is connected to your bank.

## How to choose

- **Connect a bank** if you want the least work. This suits most Households.
- **Upload a statement** if you would rather not connect, or your bank cannot be reached. Plan a few minutes every week or two.
- **Type it in** for cash, or if you like to notice every purchase as you make it.

Many Households connect their main checking account and cards, and use Quick Add for cash.

## Choosing during Setup

The first step of Setup asks how spending will come in: **Connect a bank**, **Upload a statement** or **I'll add things by hand**. If you choose a bank or a statement, Noodle reads your spending in the background while you go on, and offers amounts marked "Suggested from your spending".

> For a statement in Setup, about three months is a good amount to upload.

## Connecting a bank later

1. Go to **Accounts**.
2. Choose **Connect your bank**.
3. Answer how far back Noodle should bring in what you spent: this month only, or the last 30, 60, 90, 120 or 365 days.
4. Choose **Continue to your bank** and log in.
5. Say which Account each bank account is. Pick one you already have in Noodle, add it as new, or leave it out.

> You cannot change how far back a connection goes afterwards. Older spending stays at your bank.

## Uploading a statement later

1. Go to **Accounts**. If the Account is not there yet, choose **Add Account** first.
2. Open the Account.
3. In **Statements**, choose the file from your bank and upload it.

If the columns of a CSV file look wrong, **Columns look wrong?** lets you say which column is which.

## Adding by hand

Quick Add is in the middle of the bar on a phone, and in the sidebar on a computer. Type the amount, add a note if you like, and tap a Bucket.

Two more ways to add without typing are on **Household settings**, under Bringing in spending.

- **Forward receipts.** Get an address, then forward receipt emails to it. Noodle attaches each one to its Transaction and can split it across your Buckets.
- **Tap to capture.** An iPhone Shortcut that adds a Quick Add each time you pay with Wallet. The Setup guide there walks you through it.

## Good to know

- When your bank's copy of something you Quick Added arrives, Noodle pairs the two so it counts once. This is called a Match.
- If your bank asks you to log in again, Noodle brings in nothing until you reconnect it on Accounts. You can upload a statement in the meantime.
- You can stop a bank connection and keep the Account. Its Transactions stay, and you keep it by hand or with statements from then on.
