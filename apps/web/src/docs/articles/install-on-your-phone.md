Noodle runs in your phone's browser, so there is nothing to download from an app store. Adding it to your home screen gives it its own icon and opens it full screen, starting on This Month. On an iPhone or iPad it is also the only way to get Nudges.

## On an iPhone or iPad

1. Open Noodle in Safari and sign in.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.
5. Open Noodle from the new icon on your Home Screen.

## On an Android phone

1. Open Noodle in Chrome and sign in.
2. Open Chrome's menu.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen**. Chrome may call it Install app.
4. Confirm, then open Noodle from the new icon.

## Turn on Nudges

A Nudge is a notification Noodle sends when something changed that you would want to know now. You turn Nudges on for each device you use.

1. Open Noodle from its home screen icon.
2. Go to **More**, then **Household settings**.
3. Under Reminders, find **Nudges**.
4. Choose **Turn on**, and allow notifications when your phone asks.

You can then choose which Nudges you get: a Bucket being spent faster than the month is going, the other Parent's Quick Adds, and Extra income arriving. You can also set quiet hours. Nudges wait until quiet hours are over.

> On an iPhone or iPad, Nudges only work once Noodle is on your Home Screen and you opened it from there. If you turn them on in Safari, Noodle tells you to add it to your Home Screen first.

## Finding your way on a phone

The bar at the bottom has Month, Transactions and Goals, with Quick Add in the middle and **More** at the end. More holds everything else: Accounts, Plan, Explore, Reports, Insights, Perks & Benefits, Ask, Check-in, Household settings and the Glossary.

## Good to know

- Each Parent adds Noodle to their own phone and turns on their own Nudges.
- If your phone says notifications are blocked for Noodle, allow them in the browser's settings for the site, then come back.
- You never see a Nudge about the other Parent's Personal Allowance.
- Noodle looks the same in the browser and from the home screen. Nothing is lost if you remove the icon.
