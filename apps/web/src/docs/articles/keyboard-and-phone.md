Noodle works with a mouse or a finger alone. These shortcuts are there if you want to go faster. The single-letter keys do nothing while you are typing in a box.

## Anywhere, on a computer

- **Q** opens Quick Add. It does nothing while a sheet or the Glossary is open.
- **Ctrl B**, or **⌘ B** on a Mac, collapses and opens the sidebar.
- **Alt T** moves the keyboard to a message that has just popped up, so you can reach its Undo. **Esc** takes you back.

## Quick Add, on a computer

1. Press **Q**.
2. Type the amount with the number keys.
3. Type a letter or two to narrow the **Find a Bucket** list.
4. Use **↑** and **↓** to move to the Bucket you want.
5. Press **Enter** to add it.

## Review, on a computer

- **→** or **Enter** confirms the card.
- **←** opens the picker to choose another place.
- **↓** skips the card for now.
- **Z** undoes the last one.
- **S** splits the card.
- **P** files it in your own Personal Allowance.
- **R** makes a Rule.

## Lists and panels

- In a list, **↑** and **↓** move between rows. **Home** and **End** jump to the first and last. **Enter** opens the row.
- **Esc** closes a panel that opened from the side.
- In the Docs search box, **↑** and **↓** move through the matches, **Enter** opens one and **Esc** clears it.

## On a phone

- **Quick Add** is the button in the middle of the bar at the bottom, in reach of either thumb.
- **More** holds the pages that do not fit in the bar, and your theme and account.
- In **Review**, swipe the top card right to confirm it, or left to pick another place. A swipe that falls short springs back. Every swipe has a button that does the same.
- A message that pops up can be swiped away.

## Good to know

- To get Nudges on an iPhone or iPad, add Noodle to your Home Screen: press Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open Noodle from there.
- On an iPhone, Tap to capture adds a Quick Add each time you pay with Wallet. Set it up in Household settings, under Bringing in spending.
