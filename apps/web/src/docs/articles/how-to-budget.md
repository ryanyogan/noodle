Noodle layers a simple monthly Plan over your real spending. You set the Plan once, adjust it when life changes, and spend a few minutes a week keeping it true. This page walks through each part in order.

## Start with Income

The Plan is built on your **Take-home pay**: your usual monthly pay after taxes and deductions. It is what lands in your account.

If someone's pay varies, enter the amount you can count on: the lowest it usually is. Then a better month is a nice surprise, not a hole in the Plan.

Money that comes in beyond your Take-home pay is **Extra income**: a third paycheck, a bonus, a tax refund. Noodle never spends it for you. You decide: send it to a Goal, send it to a Bucket, or add it to that month's Free to Spend.

In a low month you can lower Take-home pay for just that month, so the Plan matches what really came in.

## List your Commitments

A **Commitment** is a bill you expect every time: the mortgage, insurance, daycare, a subscription. It can be monthly, every two weeks, or once a year.

Add every one you can think of. Small subscriptions count. Each Commitment shows its yearly cost, which can be an eye-opener.

A yearly bill, or a bill paid every two weeks that lands three times, makes a **Lumpy month**: a month with less Free to Spend than usual. Noodle shows these ahead of time so they are not a shock.

Day-to-day spending that happens to repeat, like groceries or coffee, is not a Commitment. That belongs in a Bucket.

## Set up Buckets

A **Bucket** is a monthly amount for one kind of day-to-day spending: Groceries, Fuel, Fun, Hockey. Every purchase is filed in a Bucket, and the Bucket shows how much is left.

Each Bucket works one of two ways.

- **Resets monthly.** It starts each month at its amount. Anything left over can be sent to a Goal at the end of the month. New Buckets work this way.
- **Carries over.** What you do not spend moves into next month. If you overspend, that comes out of next month. Good for things that come in bursts, like clothes or car repairs.

Each Parent can also have a **Personal Allowance**: a Bucket of their own. The other Parent sees its totals, never the individual purchases.

Start with a handful of Buckets. Too many makes filing a chore. You can add more when you see a pattern.

### When a Bucket runs over

It happens. **Cover** it: move money from another Bucket, or from Free to Spend, to bring it back to zero. Nothing leaves your bank account. You are only changing the Plan to match the month you are having.

## Add Goals

A **Goal** is a target amount you fund over time, with a date if you like. Most Goals save: an emergency fund, a holiday, a new roof. The money for a saving Goal is **Set aside** on one of your real checking or savings Accounts, so you can see how much of that balance is already spoken for.

A Goal can also **pay off a card or loan**. It follows what you owe as it comes down, and it is paid off when that reaches $0.

Goals are funded from Free to Spend. Even a small monthly amount adds up.

## Watch Free to Spend

**Free to Spend** is Take-home pay that is not planned for a Commitment, a Bucket or a Goal yet. Plan more for something and it goes down.

What a month actually ends with is **carried over**. Money left adds to next month's Free to Spend. A month that ended short takes from it. This Month shows both parts, for example "$1,846 this month · $6,710 carried over from October".

If Free to Spend is below zero, the Plan promises more than comes in. Lower a Bucket, end a Commitment you no longer need, or slow a Goal until it is back above zero.

## Keep spending filed with Review

Spending reaches Noodle three ways: a connected bank, a statement you upload, or Quick Add by hand. Noodle files each purchase in its Bucket or Commitment for you.

When it is not sure, the purchase waits in **Review**. You confirm the guess or pick the right place. When you correct one, Noodle can remember it as a **Rule**, so the same shop is filed correctly next time.

Paying your credit card from your checking account is a **Transfer**, not spending. What you bought on the card was already counted when you bought it.

## Do the weekly Check-in

The **Check-in** is a few minutes, once a week, on a day your Household chooses. It walks you through what needs you: clearing Review, looking at Insights, and deciding leftovers and Extra income. Each Parent does their own, and each can see whether the other has done theirs.

## Close the month

When a month ends, Noodle asks you to close it, in the first week of the next month. The app says "Close October". You decide three things.

1. **Leftovers.** Where the unspent money in Buckets that reset monthly goes. Sending it to a Goal is called a **Sweep**.
2. **Extra income.** Where any undecided Extra income goes.
3. **Free to Spend.** Leave what the month ended with to carry over, or send it to a Goal.

If nobody closes the month, Noodle uses the defaults. Afterwards the month shows "How October ended".

## Change the Plan when life changes

Each month's Plan starts as a copy of the one before. When you change an amount, you choose how far the change reaches: **From this month on**, or **Just this month**. A pay rise is "from now on". A big birthday party is "just this month".

## A good rhythm

- **Once:** set up Income, Commitments, Buckets and one Goal.
- **Any day:** glance at [This Month](/docs/this-month) before you spend.
- **Each week:** do the Check-in.
- **Each month:** close the month, and adjust the Plan if something changed.
