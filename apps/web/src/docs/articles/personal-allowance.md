A Personal Allowance is money that is yours to spend each month, no questions asked. It is a Bucket that belongs to one Parent. It counts in the Plan like any other Bucket, but what you buy from it is private. It is what makes a surprise gift possible, and it takes small purchases out of the conversation.

## Where to find it

- **Plan**, on the Overview page, has a **Personal Allowances** section under the Buckets.
- **This Month** shows Personal Allowances in a group of their own.

## Add yours

Each Parent adds their own. Nobody can set it for you.

1. Open **Plan** and go to **Personal Allowances**.
2. Type what you want each month under **Your Personal Allowance**.
3. Add it.

You can also add it in the **Add Buckets** sheet, or on the "Here's your Household" page when you first join.

Until both Parents have one, the Plan shows a reminder: "Still to set" with the Parent's name.

## Change it

Open your Personal Allowance from the Plan, choose **Edit Bucket**, and change the allowance. Like any Bucket, it can reset monthly or carry over.

Only you can change your own. In the other Parent's row the Plan says who sets it.

## What is private

What you buy from your Personal Allowance is yours alone to see.

- The other Parent never sees its individual Transactions: not in lists, not in search, not in [Reports](/docs/reports), not in [Ask](/docs/ask).
- If you split one purchase between your Personal Allowance and a shared Bucket, the other Parent sees only the shared part.
- The other Parent gets no Nudge about a Quick Add in your Personal Allowance.
- A Suggestion that rests on your Personal Allowance is shown only to you, marked "Only you see this".

## What the other Parent sees

- That you have a Personal Allowance, and its allowance.
- Its totals: how much has been spent and how much is left.
- In What changed on the Plan, only "Personal Allowance changed".

So the Plan still adds up for both of you. The amounts are shared. The purchases are not.

## Good to know

- Each Parent has one Personal Allowance.
- Only you can file spending in yours, Cover it, or Cover from it.
- [Quick Add](/docs/quick-add) never offers you the other Parent's Personal Allowance.
- Things to check on the Plan never calls out the other Parent's Personal Allowance for going over.
- A Personal Allowance is not archived or deleted. To stop using it, set it to $0.
- Agree the amounts together. The privacy is about what you buy, not about how much.
