Noodle is built for two Parents sharing one Plan. Almost everything is shared: the Plan, the Accounts, the Buckets, the Goals and the spending. The one private place is each Parent's **Personal Allowance**.

## What a Personal Allowance is

A Personal Allowance is a Bucket that belongs to one Parent. You see every purchase in your own. For the other Parent's, you see totals only: how much went, never what it went on.

## What the other Parent sees of yours

- **On This Month and the Plan:** the allowance and how much has been spent. Not the purchases.
- **On the Bucket's own page:** a note that it is a Personal Allowance and only its Parent sees what is spent from it.
- **In Reports:** the total, shown with a lock. It cannot be opened to see the Transactions behind it.
- **In the Log, under Logs in Household settings:** only "Personal Allowance changed", and none of the Rules that file into it.
- **In a data download:** only the total for each month.
- **In Ask:** Ask can use the total, never the purchases. See [Ask](/docs/ask).

## Other places it holds

- **Nudges.** You can be told about the other Parent's Quick Adds, but never anything in their Personal Allowance.
- **Quick Add.** You are never offered the other Parent's Personal Allowance as a place to file.
- **Suggestions.** One that rests on your Personal Allowance is shown only to you.
- **Plan health.** It never warns you about the other Parent's Personal Allowance.
- **Editing.** A Transaction with part of it in a Parent's Personal Allowance can only be changed by that Parent.

## What is not private

- Everything else. Spending in shared Buckets, Commitments, Goals and Accounts is seen by both Parents.
- The size of a Personal Allowance and its totals are part of the shared Plan.
- Snapshots hold both Parents' data so the Household can be restored. What is in them is never shown or downloaded.

## Good to know

- To keep a purchase private, file it in your own Personal Allowance. In Review on a computer, pressing P does this.
- The Check-in Nudge and email are written for each Parent separately.
