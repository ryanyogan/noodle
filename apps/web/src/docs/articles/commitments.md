A Commitment is a bill you expect every time: the mortgage, insurance, daycare, a subscription. You tell Noodle the amount and the schedule once. From then on the Plan takes it out of each month before your Buckets, and Noodle shows you what is due and what has been paid.

## Where to find it

Open **Plan**, then **Commitments**. Bills due soon also show on This Month.

## Add a Commitment

1. Open **Plan**, then **Commitments**.
2. Type its name under **New Commitment**, like Mortgage.
3. Enter the **Amount due** each time.
4. Choose its schedule: Monthly, Every two weeks, or Yearly.
5. Pick a day it is due. The rest follow from it.
6. If it pays down a credit card or loan, choose which under **Pays down**. Otherwise leave it at Nothing.
7. Choose **Add Commitment**.

## Reading the page

- **Due this month** lists the Commitments with a payment in this month. **Not this month** holds the ones that are not due, like a yearly bill in another month.
- The totals show **Expected this month**, **Paid so far** and **Still to pay**.
- A Commitment paid every two weeks or yearly also shows a month's share, so you can compare it with the monthly ones.

A payment that comes in from your bank or a statement is filed in its Commitment for you once a Rule sends that merchant there. Until then it waits in Review, where you pick the Commitment. See [Review and Rules](/docs/review-and-rules). If you keep things by hand, choose **Record payment** on the Commitment and enter the amount paid.

When a payment is more or less than expected, the row says by how much.

## A Commitment's own page

Choose a Commitment to open it. You see:

- what it costs a year, each payment, and its schedule,
- when it is next due, and whether that is paid,
- its payments over recent months,
- its terms history: what the amount and schedule were before.

**See what ending it frees up** opens [Explore](/docs/explore) with the Commitment ended, so you can look before you decide. Nothing in your real Plan changes there.

## Change or end one

1. Open the Commitment and choose **Edit Commitment**. On a computer the button reads **Edit**.
2. Change the name, amount, schedule or next due date, and save.
3. To stop it, choose **End**.

Ending takes it out of the Plan from that month on. Earlier months keep it, so your history does not change.

## Coming up

Coming up is the list of Commitments due from today through the next 30 days, by due date. Each one shows as paid, partly paid or due, from the payments recorded against it.

## Lumpy months

A yearly Commitment, or one paid every two weeks that falls three times in a month, makes that month cost more. Noodle calls it a Lumpy month. The Commitments page tells you how many are ahead, for example "2 lumpy months ahead", and links to the Plan's Year page, where each one is listed.

## Suggested Commitments

When Noodle sees the same charge keep coming back, it may offer "Netflix looks like a Commitment" with **Add Commitment**. When a bill's amount has changed, it may offer the new amount. **Not now** puts either away.

## Good to know

- Add every one you can think of. Small subscriptions count, and their yearly cost can be an eye-opener.
- Day-to-day spending that happens to repeat, like groceries or coffee, belongs in a Bucket. See [Commitments or Buckets: which is which](/docs/commitments-or-buckets).
- Paying a credit card you use every day is a Transfer, not a Commitment. A set payment on a balance you are carrying is a Commitment. See [Paying off debt](/docs/paying-off-debt).
- Changing an amount reaches that month and later months, or just that month, as you choose.
