# Desktop review (#47)

On 2026-09-30 we went through every page under `apps/web/src/routes/_authed` as a Parent who is new to budgeting. We used the `fresh` and `busy` seeds (#46), signed in as Alex. For each page:

- Screenshots at 1280, 1440 and 1920 wide, plus 393 as the phone baseline, in light and dark.
- axe-core checks (WCAG 2.2 AA rules).
- Layout shift measured on load and after scrolling.
- Horizontal overflow and clipped text.
- The h1 count and landmarks.
- Dialog probes on every sheet: does focus go in, is Tab trapped, do Esc, an outside click and Back close it, does focus return, and what happens on an empty submit.

We also walked the main flows by keyboard. The screenshots and raw metrics were kept outside the repo.

Each row is one page × one issue. **Phase** says which part of #47 fixes it:

- 2: terms (plain words, ADR-0018)
- 3: payoff Goals (ADR-0019)
- 4: flows, dialogs and duplication
- 5: shadcn components
- 6: layout, polish and accessibility

**Status** is `open` until a later phase changes it to `fixed (<sha>)` or `won't fix: <reason>`.

In the tables, paths are under `apps/web/src` unless they start with `packages/`. `R/` stands for `apps/web/src/routes/_authed/_household`.

### Bank connection, Match and statements

**Is it clear what connecting does?** Only partly. The empty-state line ("Connect a bank or card, and Noodle adds its Accounts and brings in their recent Transactions") is accurate, but it is all there is. Nothing says it's read-only, how far back it reads, that it refreshes daily, that Quick Adds get paired, or that each login uses one of 10 slots (ADR-0017). A toast is the only confirmation. In busy, Connect is a ghost button under the list, while Add Account is the header's primary button.

**What happens to Transactions entered by hand (Matches)?** The engine is right: after every Import (statement or bank), `autoMatches` pairs Quick Adds with bank lines of the same amount within −1..+5 days, and the import summary says "1 Matched to a Quick Add". The UI hardly shows it:
- Transactions has a quiet "· Matched in X" suffix, and a waiting Quick Add looks settled.
- A bank copy with a different amount (a tip) goes to Review as a new charge, and the Review card doesn't offer the Match, so confirming it counts the spend twice.
- The only manual Match is "Possible match" in the Transaction editor. For a Quick Add with no Account it lists unrelated charges from any card within ±30% (5 wrong candidates for the Costco Quick Add).

**How does connecting relate to hand-added Accounts and to statements?** It doesn't. Connecting always inserts new Accounts (seen live).
- A hand or statement Account for the same bank stays as a separate Account with its Goals, and overlapping data counts twice.
- Connected Accounts still offer Upload statement and Update balance, and list bank syncs under "Statements" ("From Ally Bank").
- A connection needing a new login (Ally) shows only on the Bank Connections row. The Ally savings and Honda loan pages don't mention it, and the Honda page tells you to upload a statement.

**Proposed changes** (spec each; add an ADR next to 0017):
1. **One "Add an Account" flow** with three choices: Connect your bank (recommended), Upload statements, Type in a balance. Each says what it does and what happens to Quick Adds.
2. **Link to existing Accounts after connecting.** After Link, "Which of these do you already have?" pairs each bank account with an existing hand or statement Account of the same kind, or makes it new.
   - A paired Account keeps its id, Goals and history, and the bank import starts after its last imported date.
   - The statement dedupe also compares date + amount + description with bank lines.
3. **Show the source on every Account** (Connected / Needs login / From statements / By hand).
   - Connected: hide Upload and Update balance, and rename the list "Brought in from <bank>".
   - Needs login: Reconnect on the Account as well, with statement upload as the stop-gap.
4. **Connect with an explainer and a result.** A pre-Link Dialog says what is read, how far back, how often, that Quick Adds are paired, and that it uses one of 10 slots. Afterwards, a result card shows Accounts added, Transactions, Matched, and waiting in Review, with a button.
5. **Put Match in the flow.**
   - Review cards offer a possible Match.
   - Transactions marks "waiting for bank" and "Matched".
   - An optional "Paid with" Account on Quick Add narrows candidates.
   - Amount-differs candidates need a merchant match, and at most 3 are shown.
   - Matching keeps edits typed in the editor.
6. **Statements feed the balance.** Offer "Update balance to the statement's $X" after an upload. Fix the card double negative. Keep the file and mapping if the sheet is closed by accident, and collapse the mapping behind "Columns look wrong?".
7. **Explain Match where it first appears** (Term popover and Glossary): "When the bank's copy of something you Quick Added arrives, Noodle pairs them so it counts once."
