# Forwarded Receipts arrive through Email Routing, and the model only reads them

A Parent forwards a receipt email to the Household's Receipt address, `receipts+<key>@<domain>`. Cloudflare Email Routing (with subaddressing on) delivers `receipts@<domain>` to the Worker's `email()` handler, so the whole path stays on Cloudflare (ADR-0005): no mail provider, no webhook, no polling.

The handler does as little as the capture endpoint does. It finds the Household by the key after the `+` (random, 100 bits, stored on `households.receipt_address`; making a new one replaces it, so the old address stops at once) and checks the envelope sender is a verified Clerk address of one of that Household's Parents. Anything else is rejected back to the sender. The raw email goes to R2 (`receipts/<household>/<id>.eml` in the statements bucket) and a small message goes on the ingest Queue the iPhone Shortcut's captures already use. The Receipt's ID is derived from the Household and the email's Message-ID, so a redelivered email is one Receipt.

The Queue's consumer reads the email with postal-mime and picks what the Receipt is: an attached PDF (turned into text by Workers AI's `toMarkdown`), else an attached picture (sent to the model as an image; its 320px WebP thumbnail made with the Images binding), else the email's own text. The `classify` model (ADR-0012) reads the lines as printed, with a Bucket and For for each item chosen from the ones the sending Parent may assign to. It never does arithmetic. `@noodle/domain` parses every amount, checks the lines add up to the total to the cent, keeps each discount with its item, and shares tax and fees across the Buckets in proportion (largest remainder, in BigInt). If they don't add up, nothing is proposed.

The Receipt is attached to the Transaction it matches (same amount, within the Match window, the one clear candidate or the one its merchant picks out), or a Quick Add is made for it (`captured_via` "receipt") and Matched with its bank copy as usual. Its Splits are applied on their own only when the model was sure of every item and nobody has decided the Transaction's assignment yet (not split, and unassigned or only as categorization filed it), by a guarded write that does nothing once a Parent has decided; otherwise a Parent applies them from the Transaction's detail. Deleting the Transaction leaves the Receipt unattached (`on delete set null`).

A Receipt's lines can name the sender's Personal Allowance. The other Parent reads a Receipt only through a Transaction they may change, and its lines' Buckets are redacted for them, as everywhere else (ADR-0003).

Considered: a third-party inbound mail webhook (another provider and secret for something Email Routing does natively), parsing in the email handler (the handler has a tight time budget and no retries; the Queue has both), and having the model produce the Splits (it gets sums wrong; the domain can check and share out exactly).
