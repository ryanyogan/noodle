# Layered Plan instead of zero-based budgeting

Amended by [ADR-0040](0040-plan-on-pay-you-can-count-on.md) (2026-10-04): a Parent may now add Extra income (the Windfall below) to that month's Free to Spend. The rest stands.

Amended by [ADR-0054](0054-free-to-spend-is-carried-over.md) (2026-10-05): a month's Free to Spend also includes what the months before it carried over, whether money left or a shortfall.

The Plan splits a fixed monthly Baseline into Commitments, Buckets, and Goals, with the remainder shown as Free to Spend. We rejected YNAB-style zero-based budgeting (assign every dollar as it arrives) because its weekly upkeep is why households abandon it, and pure tracking (Copilot-style) because it never says "stop". Only Buckets need day-to-day attention; income above the Baseline becomes a Windfall that is allocated deliberately rather than silently inflating the Plan.
