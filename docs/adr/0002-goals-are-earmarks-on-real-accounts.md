# Goals are Earmarks on real Accounts

A Goal's money is a claim (Earmark) on part of a real Account's balance, not a separate bank account and not a free-floating tracker. This keeps Goals honest against real balances without forcing the Household to open an account per Goal, and gives Affordability Checks a trustworthy picture of what is spoken for. Consequence: a withdrawal from an Account holding Earmarks must be attributed to a Goal or to Unclaimed money.
