# One bar for the whole app, and the Month glance

The Parent said This Month needed "easier visuals" and that the bars were hard to read (#64). The old Bucket meter drained to the left as money was spent, while every other bar (Goals, payoff, income, an Account's split) filled. Its hatching showed any gap past Pace, but the "Ahead of pace" badge waited for a 3% tolerance, so the two could disagree. A paragraph had to explain it.

**The one bar rule.** Every bar fills as money is used up or progress is made, with the amount and what it means written beside it. There is one component for it, `BudgetBar` in packages/ui. A Bucket fills with what's spent out of Available, in its colour. The Today line marks where even spending would be by now. Past Today, the fill turns marigold and striped when the Bucket is ahead of Pace. When it's over, the whole bar is the over ink, striped. The bar never works out the state itself: it is handed the Bucket's status from the domain, which is the same status the badge reads, so the bar and the badge cannot disagree. Every bar is 8px and rounded. A bar is a `role="meter"` with its value in words, unless the words beside it already say the value and it sits inside a link. Then it is decorative. Meter and Progress were folded into it. The Plan waterfall's floating steps and the Reports rows use it too. A test in apps/web fails on a hand-drawn bar or on an import of Meter or Progress. Reports' recharts charts are charts, not bars, and stay as they are. A small key (Spent, Left, Today, Ahead of pace) replaces the paragraph. It shows the first three times on a device, then moves into the Pace "?" popover (ADR-0018).

**The Month glance.** The Free to Spend card has the big number, then one sentence made from the numbers ("$640 free to spend with 12 days left. Groceries is running ahead."), then one stacked bar of where take-home pay goes: Commitments, spent from Buckets, left in Buckets, Goals and Free to Spend. Its inks are neutral, so colour still means only a Bucket or a Pace state (ADR-0008). Its legend is a named list with amounts, and that list is the text alternative for the bar.

**Considered and not built.** The ticket also suggested a month timeline (today, paydays and Commitments still due as dots). It was not prototyped. Coming up already lists what's due, and a second picture next to the stacked bar would have competed with it. It can come back if the Parents ask what's coming more than how the month is going. The stacked bar does not yet split Commitments into paid and still to pay.

**Colours.** Bucket colour 2 was red, close to the over ink, so an ordinary fill looked like an alarm. It is now plum. Every Bucket colour is at least 3:1 against the bar's track in light and dark (see ADR-0008). Marigold on the light track is below 3:1, so the stripes and the badge carry "ahead" there, not the hue.
