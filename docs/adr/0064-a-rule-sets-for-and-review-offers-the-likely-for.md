# A Rule sets For, and Review offers the likely For

For is quicker to set in two ways (#155). A Rule sets For as well as where it files: its For is kept as `rule_for` rows, one per Member, the shape a Transaction's For has (ADR-0011), and no rows means the Rule says nothing of who. Review offers the **likely For**: who a merchant's earlier Transactions were For. We rejected a "for" column on `rules` for the reason ADR-0011 rejected one on Transactions, and rejected writing the likely For when a line arrives: a guess written by itself would be indistinguishable from what a Parent said, in Reports › People and in the next guess.

## Consequences

- A Rule always files somewhere (ADR-0030); For is optional beside it. There is no Rule that sets only For: the form, the "Always file …?" offer and `rules`' CHECK all rest on a Rule having a Bucket or a Commitment, and a line with no place to go waits in Review anyway, where the likely For covers it.
- A Rule's For lands in the same batch that files the Transaction, on arrival and when the Rule is applied to what waits, and only on a Transaction that is For nobody yet. A Rule with no For writes no rows, so the line stays as it was (Everyone, on a new one). A Rule into a loan's Commitment that files by amount (#153) sets its For on whichever loan the payment goes to.
- A Member who has left (`members.removed_at`) is left out of a Rule's For when it is read and again in the statement that writes For, so the Rule goes on filing For whoever is left, or nobody in particular. The `rule_for` row stays; nothing is rewritten when a Child is removed.
- The likely For is a pure function (`likelyFor` in `packages/domain`): of the merchant's latest 6 filed Transactions, at least 2, every one For exactly the same Member or Members, and not Everyone (which a Transaction is anyway). "The same merchant" is told as a Rule tells it (`ruleKeys`). Whole Transactions only: a Split's For is its own.
- It is read with Review, for the Parent looking (`loadReview`), from Transactions they may read one by one (`visibleTo`), so what the other Parent filed in their Personal Allowance neither makes a suggestion nor unsettles one (ADR-0003). The two Parents can therefore be offered different things for the same card.
- The card starts with the likely For picked and says so ("For Maya, like last time", with "Not this time"). Nothing is saved until a Parent files the card; "Confirm all" files each card with what it shows. A card that is already For someone is offered nothing.
- It costs one more read each time Review loads: the Household's latest 3,000 filed Transactions, matched in the Worker. That is about a year for one family; a merchant whose earlier Transactions are all older than that is offered nothing.
